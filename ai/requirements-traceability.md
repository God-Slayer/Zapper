## Scope notes

- The assignment's sandbox and production Acme hosts end in `.example`; they are placeholders. The local service uses the Acme stub, and no live gateway call was made.
- The assignment does not require AWS deployment or infrastructure as code. The AWS material is a reasoned deployment approach in `SOLUTION.md`, not a deployed environment.
